02 / VIDEO

AV-first display mode

The launcher starts in interlaced 480i (NTSC) or 576i (PAL) for composite AV compatibility. L2+R2 switches the launcher to 720p for compatible Component or HDMI adapters. The console RGB/YPbPr setting remains in the PS2 system menu.
