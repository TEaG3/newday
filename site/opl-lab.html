EMBEDDED RUNTIMETEa.G3 / OPL

OPL is inside the TEa.G3 launcher.

The user copies one file: TEa.G3.ELF. The build embeds a pinned OPL runtime and Neutrino, including the required modules. TEa.G3 installs and integrity-checks the runtime under tea_g3/runtime/ on first boot.

Try the engine-routing preview Read the OPL guide Build workflow

Verification boundary: CI proves the pinned OPL build, the TEa.G3 patch, the embedded ELF and the host-side routing tests. It does not prove gameplay or storage compatibility on a physical PS2.

What the simulator does

Shows the routing decision only. It does not mount a USB image, load OPL, emulate a PS2 or test a game.

What CI does

Builds OPL from its pinned source, applies the UL direct-boot patch, compiles TEa.G3 and checks the routing matrix.

What needs a console

Actual game start, sustained reads, memory-card behavior, controllers and stability must still be checked on physical PS2 hardware.
