Themes: twelve looks for the launcher, a selector in the setup panel, and themes anybody can make.

## New

- **Twelve themes.** Torres (the new default: prisms of light in a blue night), NULD (v1.2's look), Cristal, Aurora,
  Liquid, Onda, Orbit, Techstep, Wireframe, Neuro, Fósforo and Papel. Each has its own colours, animated background,
  loading screen, launch screen and way of marking the selected game. The layout is the same in all of them.
- **Theme selector.** START, then **Theme**: each theme is shown at once behind the panel. Saved as `[ui] tema` in
  `config.ini`. To keep v1.2's look, pick NULD.
- **Themes from folders.** A folder in `nuld/themes/` with a `theme.ini` (a name, a built-in theme as its base and
  eleven colours) and, optionally, a 1280 x 720 `fondo.jpg` is a theme of its own. The guide is in the
  [manual](https://danielnuld.github.io/nuld/manual.html#themes) and in `docs/themes.md`.
- **Theme maker.** A [page](https://danielnuld.github.io/nuld/themes.html) that builds that folder in the browser,
  with a live preview of the home screen, and downloads it as a zip.
- **A loading sound for the new themes**: air rising and a low piano strike in a hall. NULD keeps its own.

## Known issues

- Only Torres and NULD have been timed on a console (60 frames per second on a SCPH-75001). The other ten were sized
  by the same rules and checked on PCSX2.

## Updating

With the network cable in, START at the update notice. Otherwise copy `launcher.elf` **and** `neutrino/` from the
zip over the ones on your drive.
